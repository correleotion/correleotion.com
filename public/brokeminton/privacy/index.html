Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026. Brokeminton is a personal badminton booking and Calendar automation operated by Thanakorn Sin-on.

Information accessed

With the owner's permission, Brokeminton receives the Google account's verified email address and OAuth credentials. It uses Google Calendar access to check its known badminton event and to create an event containing the booking date, start and end times, court location and selected guests' email addresses.

The requested Calendar scope technically permits reading, creating, updating and deleting events on calendars the account owns. The current integration checks its own predetermined event IDs and creates badminton events. It does not scan the owner's calendar or automatically update or delete unrelated events.

Purpose and sharing

The account email is used to verify the intended organizer. Booking details and the selected guest addresses are sent to Google to create the event and deliver invitations. Google may show the event and attendee list to its organizer and guests according to Calendar settings. The separate booking-result email sent through Resend contains the booking date, time, account alias and outcome; it does not include Google OAuth credentials or Calendar API response data.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or supplied to an AI model. Google API data is used only for this Calendar integration, in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in private files on the owner's computer and server with owner-only file permissions. They are excluded from source control and Docker images. Booking reports and diagnostic records are kept on the owner's server until manually removed. Diagnostics record outcomes and error types, not passwords, tokens or raw Calendar responses.

Events and invitation responses remain in Google Calendar until the organizer or guest manages them there. Revoking app access does not remove previously created events or invitations.

Your choices

The owner can revoke Brokeminton's access at Google Account connections and delete its local credentials. Guests can accept or decline invitations and manage their own calendar copies. To request deletion of locally stored information or ask about this policy, contact thanakorn.sinon@gmail.com.

Public information pages

These Brokeminton information pages do not run analytics or request Google sign-in. The website host may process normal web request information to serve them.
