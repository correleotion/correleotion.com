Personal badminton assistant

Brokeminton

Confirmed court bookings, shared with friends through Google Calendar.

Brokeminton is a private automation operated by Thanakorn Sin-on. It checks a fixed badminton booking plan and, once both court slots are confirmed, creates one Google Calendar event for 17:00–19:00 in Asia/Bangkok and invites the selected friends.

How Google Calendar is used

The owner connects their Google account using OAuth. Brokeminton verifies the account email, checks for its existing event to avoid duplicates, and creates the event with email invitations to the selected guests. It runs on the owner's server without an AI model reading Calendar data.

The Google permission covers viewing, creating, changing and deleting events on calendars the owner owns. The current integration uses it to check and create its badminton events; it does not automatically modify or delete other events.

Terms of use

This is a personal tool for the owner's booking plan and invited friends, not a public booking service. Availability depends on the court provider and Google. A calendar invitation represents the booking information known to the tool; it does not replace the court provider's confirmation or rules.

Changes or cancellations may need to be handled manually. Guests can accept or decline invitations in their calendar. The owner can stop the integration by revoking its access in their Google Account settings.
